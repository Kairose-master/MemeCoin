# Pump.fun description draft

SCAM is the meme token associated with the SCAM Museum. Play three fictional smart-contract puzzles, suggest a future exhibit, or report a site issue. Hold at least 1 SCAM to open an optional bonus exhibit through a read-only Phantom balance check (no signature or transaction). The museum does not detect live scams or audit contracts. No intrinsic value or promised returns; the token can go to zero. The creator's initial launch buy was burned; the creator may hold tokens acquired separately.
