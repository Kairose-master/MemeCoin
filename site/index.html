Catalog

Twelve exhibits, seven broken

Catalog numbers are the order the prover ran them. "Unproven" means no reproducible violation was found within the search budget. It does not mean safe.

SCAM-001PROVEN

ReentrantVault

Reentrancy · CEI violation

withdraw() sends ether with an external call before it zeroes balances[msg.sender]. A receive() that calls withdraw() again drains the float while the balance still reads full. The DAO, 2016, in one function.

Broken invariant: vaultSolvent
SCAM-002PROVEN

OpenVault

Broken access control

adminWithdraw(to, amount) and setOwner(newOwner) have no onlyOwner. Anyone empties the vault or takes ownership. The most common critical finding in audits, and the cheapest to fix.

Broken invariants: ownerUnchanged, vaultSolvent
SCAM-003PROVEN

BadAccounting

Integer underflow

unchecked { balanceOf[msg.sender] -= amount; }. Subtracting from zero wraps to 2256−1. Solidity 0.8 would have caught it; unchecked turned the check off to save gas.

Broken invariant: vaultSolvent
SCAM-004PROVEN

NaiveOracle

Price oracle manipulation

Collateral is valued at the pool's instantaneous spot price. No TWAP, no cap, no delay. A flash loan moves the price for one block and an under-collateralized loan walks out.

Broken invariant: protocolSolvent
SCAM-005PROVEN

DelegateVault

delegatecall hijack

The target of delegatecall is caller-supplied. delegatecall runs foreign code against this contract's storage, so the attacker's code overwrites the owner slot. Parity wallet, 2017.

Broken invariant: ownerUnchanged
SCAM-006PROVEN

PredictableLottery

Weak randomness

The winning number is keccak256(block.timestamp, block.prevrandao) % n. Everyone in the same block computes the same value. Play only when you win.

Broken invariant: houseSolvent
SCAM-007PROVEN

OpenInitializer

Unprotected initializer

Behind a proxy the constructor never runs; initialize(admin) is the constructor. Here it can be called again by anyone, so anyone becomes admin.

Broken invariant: adminUninitialized
SCAM-008UNPROVEN

SafeVault

Checks-Effects-Interactions + mutex

Balance is zeroed before the transfer and a reentrancy guard wraps the function. The prover found no violation within budget.

House rules

What it's for

Uses, not yields

Anything that turns money in into money out is a security or a scam. These are the things a ticket does.

Submit an exhibitSend a contract, or a real incident, as a candidate. If the prover breaks it, it gets a placard with your name and the proof. Submitting burns a small amount of SCAM, so nobody is paid for spam.
Break an exhibitProduce a proof against one of the five unproven contracts and collect the bounty in SOL. Holding SCAM is not required. Requiring it would make this an investment scheme.
Vote on the next wingHolders snapshot-vote which vulnerability class the museum proves next: signature replay, flash-loan oracles, bridges. The vote sets the roadmap and pays nothing.
Patron wallWallets above a threshold can opt in to appear on the patron wall. That is the whole of what a meme community is: belonging.
Self-auditThe bot's own community screen reads SCAM like any other token, creator share, holder concentration, reply growth, security verdict, and publishes the score. The museum exhibits itself.
